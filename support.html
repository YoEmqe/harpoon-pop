Harpoon Pop: Bubble Adventure

Player support

For help, bug reports or privacy questions, email contact@emreozturk.dev.

Include your device model, operating system version, game version and stage number, together with what happened. Screenshots are helpful, but please remove personal information. Never send passwords.

Getting started

Open Play → Adventure to choose Mira or Finn and follow the story. Move with the left joystick and fire upward with the right button. Move the joystick up or down at a ladder to climb. Settings includes left-handed controls, sound and reduced-motion options. The game interface is currently in English.

Scores and nearby play

Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android are optional for the adventure and are used for the Endless leaderboard. For nearby co-op, use two devices and grant Bluetooth/local network permission when prompted; one player hosts and the other joins using the displayed code.

Progress and advertising

Campaign progress is stored on your device. Contact support before uninstalling if you are troubleshooting a progress problem. Rewarded advertisements depend on availability and privacy choices. An ad reward is granted only after completion; if it fails, include the stage or mode in your report.
