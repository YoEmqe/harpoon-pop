Harpoon Pop: Bubble Adventure — iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad version of Harpoon Pop: Bubble Adventure (com.harpoonpop.bubbleadventure), developed by Emre Öztürk.

Progress on your device

The game saves progress, scores, unlocked looks and preferences on your device. No separate Harpoon Pop account is required to play the adventure. We do not operate a server for storing your campaign progress. Uninstalling the game removes its local app data; device backup and transfer settings are controlled by Apple.

Game Center

If you use Apple's Game Center, the game submits your best Endless Mode score to its leaderboard. Apple processes your Game Center identity, score and leaderboard information. Your player alias and score may be visible to other players according to Apple's settings. You can manage Game Center through iOS Settings. We do not receive your Apple Account password.

Nearby cooperative play

Nearby play exchanges session connection information and gameplay state between the two participating devices using Bluetooth and local connectivity. Bluetooth and local network access are used to connect the game session. The game does not provide player chat, and we do not operate an online matchmaking or co-op relay server.

Advertising and choices

The game includes Google AdMob advertising: a banner in the Play menu, occasional full-screen ads on returning home, and optional rewarded ads. Rewarded ads can skip an eligible Adventure level with one star or continue an Endless run up to twice. Availability depends on your region, privacy choices and ad availability.

Before enabling ads, the game asks for an age category. In the current implementation, ads are requested only for players who select the adult category. Selecting under 18 or declining to provide that category does not enable ads. The category is stored locally. Where required, Google's consent interface provides advertising choices; available choices can also be accessed through the game's advertising privacy settings.

When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising service providers may process IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic/performance data for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on consent, device settings and the SDK configuration. The game does not request precise GPS location. See Google's privacy policy and Google's advertising information. Apple governs any platform-level tracking permission.

Children

The game does not ask children to create an account or provide contact information. Advertising is disabled when the player selects the under-18 category or does not provide an adult category. Optional Apple services are subject to Apple's family and account controls. A parent or guardian may contact us with privacy questions.

Support, retention and requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send, and use them to respond and resolve the issue. Do not send passwords or sensitive personal information. We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle support and applicable obligations. You can request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us. Apple and Google manage service data retention and privacy requests under their policies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data or object to certain processing.
