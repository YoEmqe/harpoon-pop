Harpoon Pop: Bubble Adventure

Privacy choices and data deletion

Updated November 17, 2026. Developer: Emre Öztürk.

Local game data

Adventure progress, scores, unlocked looks and preferences are stored on your device, not on a Harpoon Pop server. On Android, open Settings → Apps → Harpoon Pop → Storage → Clear storage to erase local data. On iOS, delete the app to remove its local data. This removes local progress and settings; reinstalling or restoring a device backup may restore previously backed-up data. Manage device backups separately in your device settings.

Google Play Games

To remove Harpoon Pop scores and associated Play Games data, use Google Play Games Settings → Delete Play Games account & data, then delete data for Harpoon Pop. You can also manage your Play Games profile through Google. Deleting local app data does not delete leaderboard records. Google controls retention of platform records under its policies. Google's game-data deletion instructions.

Apple Game Center

Game Center manages your player profile and submitted leaderboard scores. Deleting the app does not delete those records. Manage your profile and privacy in Settings → Game Center, and use Apple's Data and Privacy tools for Apple-held personal data requests.

Advertising choices

Advertising is enabled only for the adult age selection. Where Google's privacy options are available, open the game's Settings and advertising privacy options to review or withdraw choices. Google AdMob processes advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions and diagnostics according to consent and SDK settings. We do not maintain a separate copy of these advertising records. Use Google My Ad Center and Google's privacy tools for Google-held information. Platform retention and deletion exceptions are governed by Google; deleting the app alone does not erase Google's records.

Request deletion of support correspondence

Email contact@emreozturk.dev with the subject “Harpoon Pop data deletion”, preferably from the address used to contact support. Tell us which support correspondence you want removed. Do not send passwords, identity documents or advertising IDs.

We can delete support messages and contact details held by us after verifying the request. We retain only information needed to handle the request or meet applicable legal obligations, for as long as those obligations require; any applicable exception will be explained in the response. We do not operate Harpoon Pop user accounts or a server storing your campaign progress. We cannot delete records independently controlled by Apple or Google on your behalf.
