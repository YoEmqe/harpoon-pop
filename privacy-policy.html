Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Google Play release of Harpoon Pop: Bubble Adventure (com.harpoonpop.bubbleadventure).

The game stores your progress, scores and preferences on your device so you can pick up where you left off. The game does not collect or share personal information, require an account, or send your saved data.

Your progress and settings stay saved until you clear the game's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Android cloud backup is disabled for this release. Your device may handle transfers between devices separately.

Children's privacy

The game is intended for players of all ages. It does not ask players, including children, for personal information. It contains no ads or in-app purchases.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, email contact@emreozturk.dev. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include and use it to respond to your request.
